# v1.4.0

The first release of KairoST, an Atari ST emulator.

- Plays Atari ST floppy games on the bundled EmuTOS. Once you import your own TOS, games start on it by default.
- A catalog of Atari ST games from TOSEC with MobyGames descriptions and LaunchBox artwork, where each set starts from a disk that works and its data disks stay behind it.
- Games start without EmuTOS's long white screen, and Skip memory check (on by default) shortens the boot.
- Every game's D-pad reaches the arrow keys and the joystick by default. Mappings follow a cascade: the ST defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, separately for landscape and portrait, and each game's settings can choose its own. Pick it from a 3x3 grid.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the keyboard, joystick and touchpad are on the bottom screen, and the D-pad moves between the screens in the library.

Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
